Visual - Account and Data Deletion

This page describes the account and data deletion process for the Visual app, offered by its developer, Macrofam Studio.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open the Visual app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings in the bottom menu.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the page.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete in the dialog.

Option 2: Request by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address registered in the app to onurerdal4455@gmail.com.

What data is deleted?

  • Your account (email and sign-in details), profile, profile photo and bio
  • Your username
  • Your comments and the notifications sent to you
  • Your follow and follower relationships
  • Your watchlists, ratings and other data linked to your profile

Timing and data that may be retained

Deletion from the app is applied immediately. Email requests are processed within 30 days. Copies in service providers' backups are deleted on their own cycles. Data that we are legally required to keep may be retained beyond this period.

For more information, see the Privacy Policy.
